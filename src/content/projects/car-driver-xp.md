  en: "Young people are the main victims of road crashes. For the second year in a row, Michelin Argentina launched a campaign to change how they drive, and together with the agency Almacén asked us for an online game. Car Driver XP is a driving runner for PC and mobile: reach every checkpoint without crashing while four real risks kick in (fatigue, worn tires, poor eyesight and health). Every obstacle cleared brings a safety tip; every crash, a consequence. The competition ran from 14 October to 23 December 2020, with weekly rankings and support from FIA Region IV, Total and Essilor."

Car Driver XP es un runner de manejo para PC y celular: hay que llegar a cada checkpoint sin chocar mientras aparecen cuatro riesgos reales (cansancio, neumáticos gastados, mala visión y salud). Cada obstáculo superado deja un consejo; cada choque, una consecuencia. La competencia corrió del 14 de octubre al 23 de diciembre de 2020, con rankings semanales y el apoyo de FIA Región IV, Total y Essilor."
