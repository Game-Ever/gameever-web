description:
  en: "El Gatito tells the story of a cat who lives on the streets of Buenos Aires. Destiny takes him on a long journey to the arid Patagonian desert, where, lost and afraid, he follows his instinct to survive, makes friends and finds the home the city never gave him. The thirteen-minute short was born from INCAA’s First Federal Animation Contest: winning it gave us the funding to produce it. It then received a special mention at the 2013 Mar del Plata Film Festival and the Best TDA Animation award at Mendoza Proyecta 2014. Later we turned it into a video game for Android: the hero crosses an endless desert, collecting fish, using power-ups and dodging dangers, with online leaderboards."
  es: "El Gatito narra la historia de un felino que vive en las calles de Buenos Aires. El cortometraje de trece minutos nació del Primer Concurso Federal de Animación del INCAA: ganarlo nos dio el fomento para producirlo. Después recibió una mención especial en el Festival de Mar del Plata 2013 y el premio a Mejor Animación TDA en Mendoza Proyecta 2014. Más tarde lo llevamos a un videojuego para Android: el protagonista atraviesa un desierto sin fin, junta pescados, usa power-ups y esquiva peligros, con rankings online."
